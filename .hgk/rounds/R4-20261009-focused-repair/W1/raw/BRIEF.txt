## Deliverable 1 — `tools/build_publication_manifest.py` (new)
CLI:
- `--commit <sha> --tree <sha>` : the published subject (defaults to the published tuple above; resolve `r3-candidate` if the SHA is not given).
- `--check`  : verify the projection; exit non-zero on any violation.
- `--write`  : emit `.hgk/ao/pub/PUBLICATION_PROJECTION_MANIFEST.json`.

The projection must contain, for **every** entry of the local evidence manifest:
`path`, `local_sha256`, `disposition` in {`PUBLIC_IN_PUBLISHED_TREE`, `PRIVATE_ARCHIVE`, `EXCLUDED_OVERSIZE`, `NOT_PUBLISHED`},
`published_blob_sha256` (when applicable, computed with `git cat-file -p <tree>:<path>`), and `reason` + `reference`
(e.g. private archive path + sha, or the exclusion rule for `.hgk/rounds/R2-…/own-derived.db` = 130.09 MB > 100 MB GitHub limit).
Also emit: `counts` (total / per disposition), `crosswalk_digest`, and `policy_version`.

`--check` must FAIL (non-zero) when any of these hold:
1. an entry claims `PUBLIC_IN_PUBLISHED_TREE` but the blob is absent from the published tree;
2. the local product paths (`src/ tools/ tests/ schemas/ skills/ docs/ dist/ fixtures`) for the published commit differ
   by even one byte from the same paths in the frozen local candidate `10cb3d31…` (product-path equality);
3. counts do not reconcile to the local manifest total (no entry silently dropped);
4. a seal/manifest that names a *different* candidate is used to assert the published subject.

## Deliverable 2 — `tools/publication_attestation.py` (new)
- `--build` : write the immutable sidecar `.hgk/ao/pub/PUBLICATION_SUBJECT_ATTESTATION.json` containing
  `commit`, `tree`, `product_digest` (sha256 over the sorted list of `path\0blob_sha256` for the product paths),
  `manifest_digest`, `policy_version`, `checker_identity`, `generated_at`, `source_manifest_seal`.
  Avoid any self-hash cycle: digest the payload, then store the digest next to it (never inside the digested bytes).
- `--verify`: recompute from the local git objects and exit non-zero if commit, tree, product_digest or manifest_digest differ.
