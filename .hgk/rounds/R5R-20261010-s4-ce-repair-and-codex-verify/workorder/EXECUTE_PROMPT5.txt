## Why this is a defect in your CE-2 repair, and what the correct shape is

Your CE-2 repair schema-checks a copy of the record with `NON_RECORD_SIDECARS` removed, minus any
key the schema itself declares. `NON_RECORD_SIDECARS` is the HASH-EXCLUSION list
(`subject_id`, `version`, `content_hash`, `schema_version`, `trace`, `_profile_meta`). Only
`PI-PKG` declares `trace`; the other three contracts do not. So the `declared` guard drops `trace`
for them and the `additionalProperties: false` rejection disappears.

The conflation is the bug: "fields excluded from the body hash" and "fields that are not part of
the record at all" are two different sets that happen to overlap on `_profile_meta`.

Fix it by separating the two concepts in `src/pipd_ls_sp/validate.py`:

1. Keep ONE constant for the hash-exclusion set (the identity fields plus the derived `trace`
   relation plus the sidecar) and have `_body_hash` and `pipeline._seal` reference it. Behaviour of
   the hash must not change.
2. Introduce a SEPARATE constant for the genuine non-record sidecars — the fields the pipeline
   emits for the operator/registry that NO schema declares. Today that is exactly `_profile_meta`.
   `validate_record` must strip ONLY this set (keeping a defensive "unless the schema declares it"
   guard is fine). `trace` must NOT be in it, so `trace` stays subject to `additionalProperties`
   for every contract that does not declare it, exactly as the published validator behaves.
3. Do not change any schema. Do not change what the pipeline emits.

Verify your understanding before you change anything: the required end state is that
`_profile_meta` on PI-PKG is still tolerated (CE-2 stays repaired) while an undeclared `trace` on
`PD-PKG`, `ECP` or `TQAEP` is REJECTED again.
