validator (loaded from the downloaded wheel) rejects the exact same records with
"Additional properties are not allowed (trace was unexpected)".

Root cause: the CE-2 repair conflated two distinct concepts in ONE constant
(`NON_RECORD_SIDECARS` = the HASH-EXCLUSION list: subject_id, version, content_hash,
schema_version, trace, _profile_meta). `validate_record` stripped every key on that list minus
the ones the schema itself declares, so `trace` was dropped for PD-PKG/ECP/TQAEP (which do NOT
declare it) and `additionalProperties: false` never fired. "Fields excluded from the body hash"
and "fields that are not part of the record at all" are different sets that only overlap on
`_profile_meta`.
